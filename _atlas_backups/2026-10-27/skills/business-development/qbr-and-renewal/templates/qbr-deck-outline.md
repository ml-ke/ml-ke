# QBR Deck Outline

**Account:** <account>  **Quarter:** <Qn YYYY>  **Presented by:** <names>  **Attendees:** <customer roles>

Sections run in this order. Rule: end every section with an agreed next step, an owner, and a date. The customer should talk for at least half the meeting.

## 1. Purpose and agenda
- Why we are here: the outcomes we agreed to review.
- Agenda and timeboxes.
- What we want from the customer in this session.

## 2. Value realized since last QBR
- The value-realization table: outcome, baseline, now, source, customer quote.
- One headline number that the customer agrees with.
- Honest note on where value has not yet landed and how we will close it.

## 3. Their goals and what changed
- The customer's stated priorities for the next period.
- What changed in their business, market, or organisation since last time.
- Open floor: what is working, what is not (let them speak first).

## 4. Roadmap and our commitments
- What we committed to last QBR and its status.
- Product roadmap items relevant to their goals.
- Support and adoption actions we owe them, with dates.

## 5. The ask
- One primary ask: renewal, expansion, or a referral.
- The case for it, tied to the value already shown.
- What we need from them to proceed (budget, sign-off, a meeting).
