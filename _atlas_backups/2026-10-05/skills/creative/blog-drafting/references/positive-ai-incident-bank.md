# Positive-AI anchor bank (Lane A)

Verified good-news anchors for the daily Lane A post (positive AI story, <=21 days old,
non-Western preferred). Start here instead of a cold search. **Every entry lists who owns
the number and what still needs a body fetch before publishing.** Re-check the date window
(<=21 days) before use: an anchor older than that needs a fresh hook or belongs in a
roundup.

Rule that keeps biting: **search snippets are not body verification.** A figure is
publishable only after the page that states it has been fetched (see the skill's
fact-checking protocol), and a scale figure must name the noun it attaches to (partner,
customer, funder, regulator) — aggregators routinely re-attribute a partner's number to
the funder.

## 1. WHO prequalification opens to CAD-TB software (25 Sep 2026) — USED 1 Oct 2026

Post: `_posts/2026-10-01-who-prequalification-cad-tb-ai-screening.md`. Everything below was
body-fetched and verified; reuse it rather than re-deriving.

- WHO release: <https://www.who.int/news/item/25-09-2026-who-announces-expansion-of-prequalification-programme-for-medical-devices>
  — PQ expands to CAD-TB software ("bringing a digital health technology into the WHO
  prequalification programme"), condoms/IUDs transfer from UNFPA to WHO, male circumcision
  devices fold into the general framework. Verbatim quotables: "an estimated 70% of
  countries report inadequate or weak regulatory systems for medicines and vaccines";
  MMC reduces men's HIV risk via heterosexual exposure by ~60%; the PQ list is "trusted
  guidance for United Nations agencies, procurement organizations, donors and national
  authorities"; quote from Dr Sylvie Briand (WHO Chief Scientist).
- WHO MDV prequal page: <https://extranet.who.int/prequal/prequalification-priority-medical-devices-mdv>
  — CAD-TB is a "pathfinder"; March 2021 guidelines were "the first time that software
  employing artificial intelligence is recommended in a WHO TB guideline"; independent
  assessment of three products; Technical Specifications Series sets minimum performance
  requirements.
- WHO 11 Jun 2025: <https://www.who.int/news/item/11-06-2025-who-approves-six-software-products-for-computer-aided-detection-of-tb-on-chest-x-ray>
  — 2024 open call, FIND independent validation platform, TAG review, **six products**
  approved, ages 15+ only, screen-positives need confirmatory testing, Kasaeva quote.
- WHO policy statement: <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240110373>.
- Prospective multi-site validation (NEJM AI): <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11737584/>
  — 1,910 analysed, 36% HIV+, 11% TB+; TB AI **87% Se / 70% Sp** at the high-sensitivity
  threshold, 78%/82% balanced; radiologists 76%/82%; **neither AI nor radiologists met the
  90% WHO sensitivity target at 70% specificity**.
- Community programme, Ebonyi + Nasarawa Nigeria (BMC Global and Public Health 2026):
  <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13445830/> — 9,585 screened, 3,166 flagged
  (33.0%), 1,336 presumptive, 1,123 sputum (84.1%), 204 Xpert+, 194 started treatment
  (95.1%), 2,367 (75% of abnormal) CVD/CRD features with only **12% referral completion**,
  199 clinically diagnosed.
- Version drift (PLOS Digital Health 2022): <https://journals.plos.org/digitalhealth/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pdig.0000067>
  — 12,890 CXRs; CAD4TB v6 AUC 0.823 → v7 0.903; qXR v2 0.872 → v3 0.906; older builds
  missed the WHO TPP; "prior to implementation CAD should be evaluated using local data".
- Incorporation bias (IJTLD Sep 2026, Kampala NTLP authors): <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42665896/>
  — 21 studies; pooled CXR sensitivity 86% (83–90) among symptom-positive vs 94% (92–96)
  in the original meta-analysis; pooled difference 6% (4–8). Accessed via `r.jina.ai` on
  the Ingenta page (PubMed is JS-gated here).
- TB burden numbers (WHO fact sheet + Global TB Report 2025 factsheet PDF): 10.7M fell ill
  in 2024, 8.3M newly diagnosed, ~2.4M missed, African Region 25% of new cases, Nigeria
  4.8% and DRC 3.9% of the global total.

## 8. Signvrse / Terp 360 sign-language avatars (Kenya) — USED 3 Oct 2026

Post: `_posts/2026-10-03-african-sign-language-avatar-data.md`. All facts below were body-fetched;
reuse rather than re-deriving, and keep the two attribution cautions intact.

- **The hook:** expansion beyond Kenya into **Rwanda, Uganda and South Africa** plus the **start of
  two-way translation work**, demoed at **Unstoppable Africa 2026** (New York, **21 Sep 2026**).
  Sources: Birr Metrics 22 Sep 2026 <https://birrmetrics.com/google-backed-signvrse-expands-ai-sign-language/>
  and the GABI programme page <https://gabi.unglobalcompact.org/unstoppableafrica2026/Programme-2026>
  (session "Unstoppable Africans: Elly Savatia on Turning Silence Into Signal"; the quote *"you don't
  build solutions and take them to the people; you build with them"*).
- **Origin ratio (the reusable anchor):** a northern Kenya classroom with **~300 deaf students and one
  interpreter**. RAENG <https://africaprize.raeng.org.uk/2025-cohort/elly-savatia/> and the GABI page.
- **Product facts (TechCabal, 21 Aug 2025):** <https://techcabal.com/2025/08/21/signvrse-sign-language-interpretation/>
  — motion capture of skilled deaf signers after a computer-vision approach failed; dataset of **20,000+
  professionally captured sequences**; **60% urban / 40% rural** signers; every sign Deaf-validated before
  release; **30+ deaf people** shaped the product; **35% processing-time reduction** from pre-computing common
  sign combinations; progressive streaming (*"better to show a slightly simpler sign immediately than a
  perfect one after a long delay"*); one-way only at that date, two-way "on the roadmap but technically harder".
- **Corpus units — do not conflate:** **2,300+ signs** is the vocabulary (RAENG) vs **20,000+ captured
  sequences** is the recording volume (TechCabal).
- **Recognition / funding:** 2025 Africa Prize for Engineering Innovation, **£50,000 / US$67,000**, awarded in
  Dakar 16 Oct 2025 (RAENG <https://raeng.org.uk/news/kenyan-innovator-elly-savatia-named-the-2025-africa-prize-for-engineering-innovation-winner/>;
  Disrupt Africa). Google.org Accelerator: Generative AI (June 2025 cohort) — 20 organisations sharing **$30M**,
  six months pro bono + Cloud credits; Google lists Signvrse as one of **5 with impact in Sub-Saharan Africa**,
  described as "real-time, offline sign language avatars" (blog.google Africa). Signvrse also plans to
  open-source key components (Fintech News Kenya / Africa Digest).
- **⚠️ Attribution cautions:** Google has **not** disclosed the amount allocated to Signvrse (Birr Metrics); the
  **US$2M** figure is Savatia's own, reported in UN *Africa Renewal*
  <https://africarenewal.un.org/en/magazine/africas-business-leaders-push-growth-stays-continent>. State it as the
  founder's figure, never as a Google disclosure. Savatia's own age/role details come from the GABI programme page.
- **Why it matters (demand side), NGEC statement 23 Sep 2026:** <https://peopledaily.digital/news/gender-and-equality-commission-calls-for-more-sign-language-interpreters-to-protect-deaf-rights>
  — "A right that cannot be exercised because an interpreter is unavailable … remains an unfulfilled right";
  **153,361** people aged 5+ with a hearing disability (2019 census, = 0.32% of the 47,564,296 total, KNBS) vs
  **4%** of people 5+ reporting some hearing loss (2022 KDHS) — a **12.4x** definitional gap; Constitution
  Articles 7(3)(b), 54(1)(d), 120.
- **Comprehension evidence (use for any avatar/assistive-AI post):** arXiv:2508.05358 — a DGS avatar on HoloLens 2
  found *"no significant improvements in UX or comprehensibility … remained at low levels, amid missing SL elements
  (mouthings and facial expressions)"* and concludes *"personalisation alone is insufficient, and that SL avatars
  must be comprehensible by default"*. JMUI 19 (2025), Imashev et al. (10.1007/s12193-024-00442-z) — evaluation with
  deaf signers against a human interpreter, opening on the "notable lack in the signing systems evaluation by
  individuals who utilize sign language"; also the Germany comparison (~850 interpreters / ~80,000 hearing-impaired).
- **Direction contrast:** Google DeepMind **SL2T** (12 Aug 2026, <https://deepmind.google/blog/putting-sign-language-ai-into-users-hands/>)
  is sign→text, ASL→English at launch, in Gboard / Live Transcribe on Pixel 11; "more than 200 sign languages" and
  "estimated 70 million Deaf and hard of hearing people". Terp 360 is the opposite direction — complements, not rivals.

  and Oct 1 (WHO PQ). Before adding another, prefer agriculture, education, accessibility,
  energy, disaster response or open release from the tables above.
