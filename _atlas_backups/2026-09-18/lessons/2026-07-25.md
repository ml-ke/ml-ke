## What I Learned

### 1. Skill smells taxonomy (arxiv 2607.01456 — HIGH reputability, empirical study)
"From Anatomy to Smells: An Empirical Study of SKILL.md in Agent Skills" — analyzed 238 real SKILL.md files:
- 99%+ contain at least one "skill smell"; average **10.5 smells per skill**; only 1/238 smell-free
- 11 of 26 smells occur in >50% of skills
- Top smells: Rationalization Loophole (94%), Buried Gotchas (81%), Execute Without a Plan (78%), Never Asks Human (77%), No Progress Tracking (71%), Missing Caveats (71%), No Validation Step (69%)
- Smells accumulate over time; don't disappear
- ACTION: Created `skill-quality-audit` skill with full 26-smell checklist + audit process. Use for every skill created/patched/installed.

### 2. Agent Skills spec (agentskills.io — canonical)
- name: 1-64 chars, lowercase-hyphens; description: ≤1024 chars, [what]+[when]+[keywords], third person; body <5000 words
- Progressive disclosure: summary → details → references
- Structure: SKILL.md + scripts/ + references/ + assets/
- ACTION: audit our skills against this spec going forward
