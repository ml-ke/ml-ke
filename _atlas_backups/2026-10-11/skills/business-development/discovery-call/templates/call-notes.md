# Live Call Notes

The structured capture produced by `discovery-call`. Fill it *during* the call,
not after. Verbatim where it matters — the pain and the metric.

```
Account:            <name>              Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
Attendees:          <name, role, persona - ours and theirs>
Call type:          <discovery #1 / technical / multi-thread>
```

## MEDDPICC

```
Metrics:            <the number they care about, in their words + who owns it>
Economic buyer:     <name/role of the budget signer; have we met them? yes/no>
Decision criteria:  <what they will judge the options on>
Decision process:   <the steps and people between now and a decision>
Paper process:      <procurement, legal, security review, who signs, how long>
Identify pain:      <the problem + its quantified cost, verbatim>
Champion:           <who will sell for us internally; what they have done so far>
Competition:        <named alternatives AND the status quo / do-nothing>
```
