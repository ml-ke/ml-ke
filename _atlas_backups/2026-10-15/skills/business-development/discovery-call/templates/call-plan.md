# Discovery Call Plan

The pre-call one-pager produced by `discovery-call`. Keep it to a single page;
it is a thinking tool, not a dossier.

```
Account:            <name>
Contact(s):         <name, role, persona>
Stage:              <current deal stage>
Booked via:         <reply / referral / inbound - one line of prior context>
Date / time:        <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM + zone>
Duration:           <30 min unless stated>
```

## Agenda and opening

```
Opening (2 min):
  "Thanks for the time. My goal is to understand how <problem> works for you
   today and whether we can help - not to pitch. I would like to ask about
   <area 1>, <area 2>, <area 3>, then leave time for your questions.
   Anything you want to add?"

Contract: <confirm time, confirm outcome>
```
