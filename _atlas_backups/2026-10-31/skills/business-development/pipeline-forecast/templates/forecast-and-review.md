# Part B — Weekly review agenda (60 min)

Fixed order. Every week. Inspect deals, do not read the CRM aloud.

1. **Numbers first (5 min)** — commit, best case, pipeline, coverage; delta vs last
   week. No discussion yet; just the shape.
2. **Commit deals (15 min)** — one line each: amount, close date, path, and the
   evidence. Anything without a dated buyer-confirmed path drops to best case on the
   spot, in the meeting.
3. **At-risk and stalled (15 min)** — every deal flagged stalled (no stage change or
   activity for the threshold window, or a slipped close date). Decide: unblock,
   re-qualify, or remove.
4. **Best-case promotions (10 min)** — which deals earned Commit this week and the
   specific evidence that advanced them.
5. **Bottleneck (5 min)** — one coaching theme drawn from the funnel snapshot; tie it
   to the lowest-conversion stage.
6. **Actions (10 min)** — for every deal touched: a next step, a date, and an owner.

## Closing rule

No deal leaves the review without one of: a stage advance, a required dated next step,
or a drop in forecast category. Record the outcome for each in the CRM before the next
week.

## Handoffs

- Individual deal gaps -> `discovery-call`.
- Recurring loss patterns -> `win-loss-review`.
- Coverage gaps -> pipeline generation, then `account-planning` for existing accounts.
