# Outreach Sequence

The day-by-day cadence document produced by `outbound-sequencing`. Fill it in
and hand it to whoever runs the sending tool. One channel column makes the
hand-offs between email, LinkedIn, and phone explicit.

```
Sequence:        <name, e.g. "Mid-market clinics - Q3 trigger>
Audience tier:   <A / B / C>
Value prop:      <the one line from context section 6 this sequence leads with>
Sending domain:  <secondary domain>
Mailboxes:       <n>
Daily cap:       <per mailbox>
Start date:      <YYYY-MM-DD>
Suppression:     <list checked on YYYY-MM-DD>
```

## Rules baked into the schedule

- Emails stay in **one thread** — reply to the previous one, never a new subject.
- Two channels minimum; a touch on Day 15 does not replace an email on Day 12.
- No sequence touches the same person more than 3 times in a week.
- Any reply pauses the sequence and hands off to reply-handling.
- Any opt-out ends it permanently and updates the suppression list.
