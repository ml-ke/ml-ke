## Weights

The defaults below suit a **prioritization** decision. For **entry**, raise Market
attractiveness (size + growth) and Competitive intensity. For **fundraising**, raise
Segment size. Change the weights deliberately and record why.

| Criterion | Weight | Scoring guide (1 = weak, 5 = strong) |
|-----------|--------|--------------------------------------|
| Segment size (SAM) | 20% | 5 = clears your revenue bar alone; 1 = a rounding error |
| Growth rate | 15% | 5 = growing faster than the category; 1 = flat or shrinking |
| Right-to-win | 20% | 5 = existing proof/references/product fit; 1 = no angle |
| Access | 15% | 5 = reachable via a channel you already run; 1 = cold and costly |
| Willingness to pay | 15% | 5 = will pay list, pain quantified; 1 = price-sensitive, unproven |
| Competitive intensity | 10% | 5 = few credible alternatives, low switching; 1 = entrenched rivals |
| Cost to serve | 5% | 5 = fits your onboarding/support model; 1 = bespoke every time |
