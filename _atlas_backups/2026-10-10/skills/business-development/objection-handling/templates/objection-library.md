## Entry: "Too expensive"

1. **Objection (verbatim):** "Your quote is 20% more than the other vendor."
2. **True meaning:** Price family — value not yet believed, or a comparison on a
   stripped-down feature set.
3. **Acknowledge:** "Fair — that is a big gap, and I would want to understand it too."
4. **Reframe:** "The other quote excludes fatigue guardrails and statutory filing.
   What would one compliance miss cost you?"
5. **Proof:** [cost-of-a-miss figure from discovery] — source: [deal notes].
6. **Question close:** "If the comparison were equal on scope, which two features
   would decide it?"
7. **Source:** [example entry — replace with your own].

## Maintenance rules

- Add an entry the first time an objection is heard in a real deal; do not wait to
  synthesize.
- Proof must be sourced. "None yet" is valid; an invented metric is not.
- If an objection recurs more than three times, it is a positioning signal — hand it
  to `value-proposition-and-pricing` rather than sharpening the script.
- Review the win rate after response; drop or rewrite entries that do not move deals.
