# Question Bank

Detail for `discovery-call` section 3. Pick from these by situation and persona;
do not read them all. Ask open questions and let the prospect talk roughly twice
as much as you.

## SPIN, in order

### Situation (facts — ask sparingly)
- "Walk me through how <process> runs today."
- "Who owns <process>, and who else touches it?"
- "What tools or systems are involved?"
- "How has that changed in the last year?"

### Problem (surface friction)
- "Where does that break or cost you the most?"
- "What is the most frustrating part of it?"
- "How often does that go wrong?"
- "What have you already tried, and why did it fall short?"

### Implication (size the cost — this is where urgency lives)
- "What does that failure cost you a month — in hours, money, or risk?"
- "What happens downstream when it slips?"
- "How does that affect the team's other priorities?"
- "What is the risk if nothing changes this quarter?"

### Need-payoff (let them state the value)
- "If that were fixed, what changes for the team?"
- "How would that show up in your numbers?"
- "What would that free you up to do?"

### Gap selling (current vs desired, quantified)
- "Where should this be, and what is the gap between that and today?"
- "What is the cost of the gap per month?"
- "What is the root cause of the gap?"
- "What does closing it make possible?"

## By persona

| Persona | Their metric | Question that lands |
|---------|--------------|---------------------|
| Economic buyer | Cost, risk, growth | "What would make this worth the budget?" |
| Champion | Their own reputation/outcome | "What would success look like for you personally?" |
| User | Time, frustration | "What do you spend your day on that you would rather not?" |
| Technical influencer | Fit, integration, security | "What would it need to integrate with to be viable?" |
| Blocker | Risk, compliance, status quo | "What would need to be true for you to be comfortable?" |

## Process and approver questions (never skip)

- "Who else needs to agree, and what do they care about?"
- "Walk me through what happens between now and a signed contract."
- "Who handles procurement and legal, and how long do they take?"
- "What is your timeline, and what drives it?"

## Rules

- One question at a time; stop talking after you ask.
- Follow the thread the prospect opens, even if it is not on your list.
- Reflect back what you hear before moving on ("So the cost is X, driven by Y").
- Do not pitch until you can state the problem, its cost, and who owns it.
